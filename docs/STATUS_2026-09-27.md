## What the headline claim looks like now

The L3 positive holds for the agent it came from: hidden 8, G seed 0, decoder attached. It
reads 0.752 on GPU under the split as published and 0.774 under the stack-independent
split; 0.730 on CPU. The behavior-controlled signal reads 0.723 on GPU (seven-channel
basis), 0.750 under the explicit split, and 0.710 on CPU. The film's 73% is the GPU
measurement; the CPU rerun gives 71%. The fold fix moves the published numbers up, not
down, and moves no decision clause.

It needs the decoder. On one device, taking the decoder away drops survival from 0.730 to
0.601, and prediction alone reads 0.589. Whether that is the decoder itself or the survival
skill it buys is what step 10 is running to separate.

Seed and capacity are now separated, and the size of the effect does not travel. Two
independently trained fingerprints, hidden 10 at G seed 1 (0.612 on the same GPU as the
headline) and hidden 8 at G seed 2 (0.676), both land above their untrained floors and
above their predictor arms but under the 0.65 bar with the CI excluded. The direction
replicates; the magnitude is specific to the instance the claim came from. The device
explains about 0.03 of that gap, not the rest.
